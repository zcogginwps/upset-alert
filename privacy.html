Privacy Policy

Quad Box Buddy · Last updated 2 November 2026

The short version: Quad Box Buddy does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything you choose in the app stays on your own device.

Information we collect

None. The app has no sign-in, no user accounts, and no analytics or advertising software. We do not operate a server that receives your data, and we have no way to identify you or your device.

Information stored on your device

So the app remembers your preferences between visits, the following is saved locally on your device using standard browser storage. It never leaves your device and we never see it:

  • Which conference and ranking filters you have turned on
  • Games you have starred and the teams you have marked as favorites
  • Which ranking or rating you have selected
  • A short-lived cache of scores, betting lines, and game details, so the app works smoothly and does not re-request the same information

You can erase all of it at any time by deleting the app, or by clearing website data for this site in your browser settings.

Information sent to others

Scores, schedules, team records, rankings, and betting lines are requested by your device directly from ESPN's publicly available endpoints. We do not sit in the middle of that request and we do not receive a copy of it. As with loading any web page, ESPN's servers receive your device's IP address and a standard request header. Their handling of that is governed by ESPN's own privacy policy.

The app's files are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever begins collecting information — for example if advertising is added — this page will be updated before that change ships, and the date at the top will change.
